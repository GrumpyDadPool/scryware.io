The company

One studio. Zero dark patterns.

Scryware is an independent software studio run by Kevin Gillum — a systems engineer with 20+ years of experience building and maintaining the infrastructure that keeps businesses running, from retail point-of-sale networks to enterprise IT.

How it started

After two decades of enterprise IT — keeping thousands of systems healthy, secure, and compliant — the pattern was hard to ignore: the tools professionals rely on are either powerful but miserable, or pleasant but toys. ScryWare, the flagship product, grew directly out of that work: the monitoring and self-healing platform Kevin always wanted on his own networks, built for the MSPs and small businesses living the same reality.

The consumer apps came from the same instinct applied to everyday life — look at what people actually complain about, then build the straightforward thing they're asking for.

What we believe

One studio, two kinds of software — the same plain philosophy underneath.

The flagship — ScryWare

01 Know before they call

One calm dashboard for the whole fleet. No RDP-hopping between machines, no finding out from the client.

02 Fix it before it's a ticket

Queue a remediation once and the system corrects problems in real time — instead of paging you at 3 AM.

03 Assume hostile networks

Dual authentication, role-based access, agents that dial out so you never open inbound holes. Security is the architecture, not a feature list.

The apps — one belief each

01 Finance Dashboard

Clarity without custody. Net worth and bills at a glance — manual entry, no bank logins, nothing ever leaves your phone.

02 Bill Reminder

Bills shouldn't be a memory test. Every bill and subscription in one calm list, with a reminder before anything is due.

03 ADHD Focus Companion

Your brain isn't the problem. It decides what to do next and nudges without shame — praise when you finish, compassion when you're stuck, never punishment.

04 Home Maintenance

Small tasks now beat big repairs later. Seasonal checklists and a completion log that protects your home — and its resale value.
