Focus Up

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Developer: Scryware

Contact: kevin@scryware.io

Focus Up (also called ADHD Focus Companion) does not collect personal information. It has no account, no cloud sync, and no bank or finance login. What you enter stays on your phone. The app does not send it to Scryware or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

What you enter in Focus Up stays on the device. Scryware does not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
