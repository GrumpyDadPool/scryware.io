The Glances

Your Life, At A Glance

Three small apps, each built around a single screen that tells you what you need to know: where to find legal help, what your home needs next, and where your money stands. Pick a Glance to see what it does.

LegalGlance

Free

Help, without a paywall.

When someone needs legal help, they usually need it on the worst week of their year, and the first problem is embarrassingly basic: who do you even call? LegalGlance answers that with a directory of legal aid and related services that you can search by the place you actually live, right on your phone.

  • A real directory. Legal aid and related services covering all 50 states and DC, plus international listings, with names, addresses, and phone numbers kept current.
  • Search where you live. Look up help by city or ZIP code and get the services that actually cover your area.
  • Near me. If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location and works out distances on the phone, so the closest help comes first.
  • A hand from Brain. Scryware Brain runs on your device and can help you sort out what kind of help fits your situation, without your questions leaving the phone.
  • Free means free. People in need should not have to pay to find help, so there is nothing to buy in this app and there never will be.

HomeGlance

Small tasks now beat big repairs later.

HomeGlance is the simple checklist and log homeowners keep asking for. Home maintenance lives on a schedule, with reminders that land before small tasks turn into repairs, and a record of everything you have done.

  • Templated checklists. Seasonal, monthly, and yearly tasks, templated by climate zone and home type. Editable, obviously.
  • Plain-English task entry. Describe the job in your own words, and the app drafts the task for your checklist.
  • Describe a problem. Tell the app what looks wrong, and it suggests the checklist task that matches. Anything involving gas, wiring, structure, flooding, or mold gets pointed to a licensed professional instead of do-it-yourself steps.
  • Local reminders. Furnace filter, gutter cleaning, water heater flush, before they become repairs.
  • Completion log. Every finished task logged with dates, exportable as CSV or JSON. Genuinely useful at resale or insurance-claim time.
  • 100% on-device. Nothing uploaded, nothing tracked.

CashGlance

Your money, on your terms.

CashGlance is a manual finance dashboard: net worth and bills, at a look. You enter the numbers yourself, no bank login, and everything stays on your phone.

  • Manual balances. Cash, investments, and debts, entered by hand with full history charting. No bank login, ever.
  • Net-worth trend line. The direction and speed of your money, not budgeting depth you did not ask for.
  • Bill list with due dates. What is owed and when, right next to the numbers.
  • Bill entry by description. Type a bill the way you would say it out loud, and Scryware Brain drafts the entry for you to check and save.
  • Home-screen widget. Net worth and upcoming bills without opening the app.
  • Encrypted export and import. Your data leaves the device only when you say so, and only encrypted.
