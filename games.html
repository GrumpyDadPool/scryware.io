Scryware, The Fun Side!

The games.

The apps pay the bills in spirit; the games are why the studio exists on weekends. Two of them are already in testing on Android, two are live or building on Roblox, and five more are in the works as full indie games. This page is where all of that lives.

Doomsday Tycoon

In closed testing on Google Play

Build the empire. Run the lair. Try not to get raided.

An idle clicker about running a villain empire one enterprise at a time. Six lairs, each with its own ten enterprises, its own henchmen, and its own look. Hire the crew, let the money pile up while you are away, fend off the heroes, and prestige when the climb slows down. Paid boosts and shortcuts only: no premium lairs, no loot boxes, nothing held back for money.

Umbra Field

Version 2.0 in review at Google Play

Deep space, wrapped around a black hole.

A live wallpaper that generates a deep-space scene on your phone: a dense starfield with real depth, passing phenomena that never repeat the same way twice, and a black hole at the center of it all. Your battery level is written into the colour of the horizon, so the sky itself tells you how the phone is doing.

In the works

Five indie games in pre-production. Scripts, concept design, and systems first; building starts when the design is nailed down. These are honest works in progress, not announcements with dates.

Dead Air

In pre-production

A late-night radio horror game. You host the overnight show at a small station, take the calls that come in after midnight, and decide which voices to trust. The dialogue for the first episode is written; the rules of the night are not the kind that forgive careless answers.

Moonlit Market

In pre-production

A night market game about running a food stall under the lanterns. You cook for a cast of regulars, chase rare ingredients, and save up through the festival weeks, when the whole market changes its mood. Cozy on the surface, with a real economy underneath.

Road to Rudis

In pre-production

A gladiator school management game. You recruit and train fighters, keep the school solvent, and make the calls that decide who climbs and who does not, all on the road to the rudis, the wooden sword that means freedom.

Hangman's Hollow

In pre-production

A frontier horror game set in a gulch town that hanged the wrong people and has been paying for it since. Gunslingers, deadeyes, and things in the dark with too many teeth. The darkest project on the slate, and deliberately so.

Sandstorm Season

In pre-production

A desert game of caravans, forts, and the long storm season. You move people and goods between oases, build up shelter before the weather turns, and live with what the sand decides to keep. It has the strongest key art of the indie line, and the design is being built to deserve it.
