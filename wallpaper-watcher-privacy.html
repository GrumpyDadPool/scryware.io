Wallpaper Watcher

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Wallpaper Watcher does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

  • No data collection. The app reads only the single image file you explicitly select with the system file picker, and only to set it as your wallpaper. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or sent anywhere.
  • No network access. The app requests no network permissions and makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, ads, or trackers. There are none in the app.
  • On-device only. Your file selection and settings (which screens get the wallpaper, whether watching is enabled) are stored in the app's private preferences on your device and never leave it.

Questions about this policy: kevin@scryware.io
