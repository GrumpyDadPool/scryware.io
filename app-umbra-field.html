Umbra Field

Umbra Field is a live wallpaper that turns your home screen into deep space. The scene is generated on your phone: a dense starfield with real depth, wrapped around a black hole whose glowing horizon is the center of everything. It is built around the blackness. The shadow at the middle is not a picture pasted on top; it is the thing the light bends around.

Version 2.0 in review at Google Play

Deep space, wrapped around a black hole.

Android · Live wallpaper

Demo video coming soonA short capture of the wallpaper running on a home screen will be embedded here.
  • A living starfield. Thousands of stars with depth and drift, generated procedurally on your phone rather than played back from a video.
  • Events, not loops. The field is not a recording. Things happen out there: phenomena cross the sky, play out, and move on, and the sky is never quite the same twice.
  • Your battery, written in light. There is no battery meter and no percentage readout. The colour gradient of the horizon around the black hole shifts with your battery level, so the scene itself tells you how the phone is doing.
  • Your sky, your rules. A settings screen lets you choose which phenomena can appear, so the wallpaper behaves the way you want it to.

Privacy

Umbra Field collects nothing. The battery level it reads stays on the phone and is used only to drive the display. The full wording is in the Umbra Field section of the privacy policy.

How to get it

Version 2.0 is in review at Google Play, and the current build is available through closed testing in the meantime. Join the Scryware Testers group first, then opt in below and install it from the Play Store. One caution from experience: set it as your wallpaper and let Android keep it; force-stopping a live wallpaper stops the sky with it.
