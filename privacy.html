This page is the privacy policy for the Scryware Android apps named below. It replaces the separate pages for Finance Dashboard (effective October 4, 2026), FocusUp (effective October 5, 2026), and Wallpaper Watcher (effective September 30, 2026). Those addresses now redirect here.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Finance Dashboard

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Finance Dashboard does not collect personal information. It has no account, no bank login, no ads, and no analytics. The balances, debts, and bills you type in stay on your phone. The app does not send them to Scryware or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

You enter account balances and bills yourself. The app stores them in a private database on the device, along with the history you keep so it can show a trend. A home-screen widget can show your net worth, the date of the latest figure, a short change, and the name and date of the next bill. That widget only displays what is already on the phone.

Reminders are off until you turn them on for a bill. A reminder is a notification on your phone with the bill's name and date. It does not include the dollar amount. If the app was set to remind you, it can start those reminders again after a reboot. Nothing in the notification is sent off the phone.

Export

You can save an encrypted copy of your data to a file you choose, with a passphrase you choose. You can read that file back into the app later. Scryware does not receive the file or the passphrase. If you save the file to a cloud drive, that copy is governed by the drive you picked, not by Scryware.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy stored in the app.

Purchases

The app does not see your card number, bank login, or Google account password. If you buy the full version on Google Play, Google handles that payment under Google's own policies. Your balances and bills are not part of the purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data from anyone.

FocusUp

Effective date: October 5, 2026

FocusUp has no account, no cloud sync, and no bank or finance login. What you type stays on your phone. The app does not send it to Scryware.

What stays on your phone

Tasks and inbox items you type stay on the phone. Scryware does not receive them. Words from Voice Catch, and plain text shared in from another app, stay on the phone too.

Voice Catch

Voice Catch uses the microphone to catch a spoken thought for the inbox. It uses Android's on-device speech recognizer. The app does not keep the audio. It keeps the words on the phone.

Calendar

If you allow it, FocusUp reads busy start and end times from your calendar, so a long task is not the next pick when it will not fit before the next event. It does not read event titles. It does not change your calendar. Those times are used on the phone.

Sharing

Other apps can share plain text into FocusUp. That text is saved on the phone.

Reminders

FocusUp can show a reminder notification for the next step. It asks permission to show notifications. After a restart it can start those reminders again. Nothing in the notification is sent off the phone.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Wallpaper Watcher

Effective September 30, 2026

Wallpaper Watcher reads the image you pick and keeps its settings on your phone. Nothing is sent to Scryware.

  • The image. The app reads only the single image file you explicitly select with the system file picker, and only to set it as your wallpaper.
  • Settings on your phone. The app keeps its settings in private storage on the phone. That includes which file you chose, the file's name, which screens get the wallpaper, whether watching is on, and a fingerprint of the file so the app can tell when the image changed. Those settings stay on the phone.
  • No network. The app does not use the network. There are no ads or analytics.

Bill Reminder

Bill Reminder has no account, no subscription, and no bank link. The bills you enter stay on your phone. The app does not send them to Scryware or to anyone else. Unlock through Google Play is not available yet.

What stays on your phone

You enter bills yourself. The app stores them on the phone, along with payments and reminder settings. A bill holds the name, amount, how often it repeats, the next due date, a category, a note, and whether reminders are on for that bill. A payment holds the bill name, the date paid, and the amount. Snooze times stay on the phone, as does a record of when the app was first installed and whether it is unlocked.

A reminder is a notification on your phone with the bill's name, whether it renews or is due, the date, and the amount. A note the morning after uses the same fields. Snooze moves that one reminder by one day and does not change the due date. If the app was set to remind you, it can start those reminders again after a reboot. Nothing in the notification is sent off the phone.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it.

Purchases

Unlock through Google Play is not available yet. When a purchase is offered, Google handles that payment. The app does not send your bills as part of it.

Home Maintenance

Home Maintenance keeps the checklist and the completion log on your phone. There is no account. The app does not send them to Scryware.

What stays on your phone

The phone can hold the home type, the climate, the heating, whether you have gutters and a yard, task names, notes, how often a task repeats, due dates, whether a task is paused, the dates the work was marked done, which templates you use, and reminder settings. A new install starts with no checklist.

Export

You can export the log. Export opens the Android share sheet, and you choose where the file goes. The app does not upload it. The spreadsheet is completed work only: the date, the task name, how often it repeats, and the note. The other file is the one you use to put the checklist back on a phone. Importing it replaces what is on that phone.

Backup

This app's data is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. An older copy can still sit in the Google account from a backup made before this version. A new phone does not get the log unless you export it and move that file yourself.

Reminders

Reminders stay on the phone. A reminder names the task and when it is due. It does not include a dollar amount. The app asks permission to show notifications. After a restart it sets the reminders again from the tasks stored on the phone. Paused tasks stop reminding and keep their history.
