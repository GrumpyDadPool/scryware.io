This page is the privacy policy for the Scryware Android apps named below. It replaces the separate pages for Finance Dashboard (effective October 4, 2026), FocusUp (effective October 5, 2026), and Wallpaper Watcher (effective September 30, 2026). Those addresses now redirect here.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Finance Dashboard

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Finance Dashboard does not collect personal information. It has no account, no bank login, no ads, and no analytics. The balances, debts, and bills you type in stay on your phone. The app does not send them to Scryware or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

You enter account balances and bills yourself. The app stores them in a private database on the device, along with the history you keep so it can show a trend. A home-screen widget can show your net worth, the date of the latest figure, a short change, and the name and date of the next bill. That widget only displays what is already on the phone.

Reminders are off until you turn them on for a bill. A reminder is a notification on your phone with the bill's name and date. It does not include the dollar amount. If the app was set to remind you, it can start those reminders again after a reboot. Nothing in the notification is sent off the phone.

Export

You can save an encrypted copy of your data to a file you choose, with a passphrase you choose. You can read that file back into the app later. Scryware does not receive the file or the passphrase. If you save the file to a cloud drive, that copy is governed by the drive you picked, not by Scryware.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy stored in the app.

Purchases

The app does not see your card number, bank login, or Google account password. If you buy the full version on Google Play, Google handles that payment under Google's own policies. Your balances and bills are not part of the purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data from anyone.

FocusUp

Effective date: October 19, 2026

FocusUp has no account and no cloud sync. Tasks and inbox items you type stay on your phone. Scryware does not receive your tasks or settings.

Voice Catch

Voice Catch uses only the phone's on-device speech recognizer, set to prefer offline. If the phone doesn't offer one, including phones older than Android 12, the microphone button is hidden. No audio is saved. The words go into the Inbox only.

Calendar

If you allow it, FocusUp reads only today's busy start and end times. It skips all-day, cancelled, and free events. It never reads event titles, locations, or attendees. The times are used in memory to rank tasks and are not stored. It does not change your calendar.

Sharing

Other apps can share plain text only into FocusUp. That text is saved on the phone.

Reminders

Notifications are local reminders. When the phone restarts, FocusUp starts automatically to set its reminders and home-screen widget again.

Backup

Android backup is enabled, using Android's default automatic backup. That backup can include your tasks and settings. It is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it. Calendar times and audio are not stored, so they are not backed up.

Purchases

FocusUp offers a 14-day free trial and a one-time unlock through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and Scryware receives no payment details. The trial start date and whether you bought the unlock stay on the phone. The app checks the price and your purchase status with Google Play, and that is the only time it uses the network.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Wallpaper Watcher

Effective October 29, 2026

Wallpaper Watcher works on your phone, from the image you pick, and keeps its settings on your phone. Nothing is sent to Scryware. Optional features can fetch an image from a web address you enter or from a Google Drive you connect, and only at your direction, when you set that source up and choose it.

  • The image. The app reads only the image or folder you explicitly select, whether it is a file on your device, a file or folder in your Google Drive, or an image at a web address you enter, and only to set it as your wallpaper.
  • Settings on your phone. The app keeps its settings in private storage on the phone. That includes which source and file you chose, the file's name, which screens get the wallpaper, whether watching is on, and a fingerprint of the file so the app can tell when the image changed. Those settings stay on the phone.
  • Network, only for the source you choose. With the on-device source, the app makes no network requests. If you choose Google Drive, you sign in with Google inside the app; access is read-only, sign-in tokens are stored only on your phone and sent only to Google, and the app downloads only the file or folder you picked. If you choose a web URL, the app downloads the image from that address. There are no ads, analytics, or trackers.

Bill Reminder

Bill Reminder has no account, no subscription, and no bank link. The bills you enter stay on your phone. The app does not send them to Scryware or to anyone else. Unlock through Google Play is not available yet.

What stays on your phone

You enter bills yourself. The app stores them on the phone, along with payments and reminder settings. A bill holds the name, amount, how often it repeats, the next due date, a category, a note, and whether reminders are on for that bill. A payment holds the bill name, the date paid, and the amount. Snooze times stay on the phone, as does a record of when the app was first installed and whether it is unlocked.

A reminder is a notification on your phone with the bill's name, whether it renews or is due, the date, and the amount. A note the morning after uses the same fields. Snooze moves that one reminder by one day and does not change the due date. If the app was set to remind you, it can start those reminders again after a reboot. Nothing in the notification is sent off the phone.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it.

Purchases

Unlock through Google Play is not available yet. When a purchase is offered, Google handles that payment. The app does not send your bills as part of it.

HomeGlance

HomeGlance keeps the checklist and the completion log on your phone. There is no account. The app does not send them to Scryware.

What stays on your phone

The phone can hold the home type, the climate, the heating, whether you have gutters and a yard, task names, notes, how often a task repeats, due dates, whether a task is paused, the dates the work was marked done, which templates you use, and reminder settings. A new install starts with no checklist.

Export

You can export the log. Export opens the Android share sheet, and you choose where the file goes. The app does not upload it. The spreadsheet is completed work only: the date, the task name, how often it repeats, and the note. The other file is the one you use to put the checklist back on a phone. Importing it replaces what is on that phone.

Backup

This app's data is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. An older copy can still sit in the Google account from a backup made before this version. A new phone does not get the log unless you export it and move that file yourself.

Reminders

Reminders stay on the phone. A reminder names the task and when it is due. It does not include a dollar amount. The app asks permission to show notifications. After a restart it sets the reminders again from the tasks stored on the phone. Paused tasks stop reminding and keep their history.

Purchases

HomeGlance offers a free trial and a one-time unlock through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and Scryware receives no payment details. The trial start date and whether you bought the unlock stay on the phone. The app checks the price and your purchase status with Google Play, and that is the only time it uses the network.

Doomsday Tycoon

Doomsday Tycoon keeps your game on your phone. There are no ads and no analytics, and the game does not send your progress to Scryware.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress is saved on your phone. Resetting the game in Options or uninstalling the app deletes the copy stored in the app.

Play Games, only if you sign in

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and is never required to play. If you sign in, Google Play Games can keep a cloud save of your game (Saved Games) so your empire can follow you to a new phone. That save is held by Google under Google's policies, and Scryware does not receive it. If you never sign in, your progress lives only on the phone.

Backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include your saved game in the backup for your phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's policies. Scryware does not receive it.

Purchases

The game may offer optional boosts and shortcuts through Google Play. If you buy one, Google handles the payment under Google's own policies, and Scryware receives no payment details.

The game is not directed at children under 13.

LegalGlance

LegalGlance helps you find legal help. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

The directory

The directory is public listing information: the names, addresses, and phone numbers of legal aid and related services, published so people can find them. It is bundled with the app, and the app can download directory updates so the listings stay current.

Searches

Searches run on your phone against that directory. What you search for is not sent to Scryware, apart from the optional local results described below.

Local results, only if you turn it on

The Directory filters include an "Include local results" option, off by default. If you turn it on, the words in the search box are sent through a Supabase function run by Scryware to Context.dev, a web search provider, which returns local law offices found on the web. Those results are shown on your phone, marked as not verified, and neither the results nor your searches are stored. With the option off, nothing about your searches leaves the phone.

Near me

If you use the near-me feature, the app uses your approximate location and works out distances on the phone. Your location is not sent to Scryware.

Scryware Brain

Scryware Brain runs AI on your phone for the other Scryware apps. Prompts, and the data in them, are processed on the device by a local model. There is no account, and Scryware receives no prompts, keys, or results.

Cloud AI, only if you turn it on

Brain can use a cloud AI service only if you enable it and supply your own API key. It is off by default. If you turn it on, what you send goes to the provider you chose, under that provider's policies.

Umbra Field

Umbra Field collects no data. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics, and nothing is sent to Scryware.

Battery

The wallpaper reads your battery level and charging state on the phone to drive the display. That reading never leaves the device.

Settings

Your choices about what appears in the scene are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
