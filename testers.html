Closed testing

Test the apps.

Every Scryware app is in closed testing on Google Play, and Google only lets an app go public after real testers have lived with it for a while. That is where you come in. Joining takes a few minutes, costs nothing, and genuinely decides whether these apps ship.

How it works

1

Join the Scryware Testers group

One Google Group covers every app. Join it with the Google account you use on your phone: groups.google.com/g/scryware-testers

2

Opt in to the apps you want

Each app has its own opt-in link in the list below. Open the link, choose Become a tester, and Google Play will offer you the test build like any other app.

3

Install, and keep them installed

Testing only counts while an app stays installed, and Google measures it over days, not minutes. Install the ones you picked and leave them on your phone while you use them like you normally would.

4

Install Brain too, and open it once

Scryware Brain powers the smart features in FocusUp, CashGlance, HomeGlance, and LegalGlance. Install it alongside them and open it once so it finishes setting up.

The opt-in links

Join the group first, then use these. If a link tells you the app is not available, you are most likely signed in to Google with a different account than the one that joined the group. Switch accounts and try again.
