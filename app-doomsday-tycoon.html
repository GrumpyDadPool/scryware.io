Doomsday Tycoon

Doomsday Tycoon is an idle clicker about running a villain empire one enterprise at a time. You start with a small hideout and a single money-making operation. You buy more enterprises, hire the henchmen who staff them, and watch the whole operation keep earning while you are away. Every lair is its own place with its own ten enterprises, from the first back-room operation up through a skyscraper, a volcano, an orbital station, and a moon base.

In closed testing on Google Play

Build the empire. Run the lair. Try not to get raided.

Android · Free, with optional paid boosts and shortcuts

Demo video coming soonA short gameplay capture will be embedded here.
  • Six lairs, six sets of enterprises. Each lair has its own ten businesses with their own names, art, and progress bars. Nothing is reskinned from the lair before it.
  • Henchmen who do the work. Buy an enterprise and it sits empty. Hire its henchman and the place comes alive, running and earning on its own.
  • Earnings that keep their manners. Offline returns wait for you when you come back. Small returns stay quiet. Big ones get a proper count-up and a look at the next milestone you are closing on.
  • Ranks, quests, and achievements. Your villain rank rises as the empire grows, with daily quests and achievement families tracking the run.
  • Hero raids. Heroes show up to wreck your operation. Your crew is the defense.
  • Prestige. Reset the run for permanent gains and climb faster the second time.
  • Fair monetization. Paid boosts and shortcuts only, in the Adventure Capitalist style. No premium lairs, no content held back for money, and no loot boxes.

Privacy

Your game progress lives on your phone, and the game carries no ads and no analytics. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional; if you do, Google can keep a cloud save so your empire survives a new phone. The full wording is in the Doomsday Tycoon section of the privacy policy.
