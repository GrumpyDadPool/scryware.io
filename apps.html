The repertoire

Small apps, done right.

Alongside the flagship platform, Scryware builds focused Android apps with a shared philosophy: private by design, respectful of your attention, and honest about price. Most of what these apps know never leaves your phone, and where an app can use the network, it says so plainly and only at your direction. Every app below is in closed testing on Google Play right now, and each one has its own page with the full breakdown.
