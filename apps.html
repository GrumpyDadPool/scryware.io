Finance Dashboard

In development

Your money, on your terms.

Android-first · One-time purchase

After Mint shut down, its successors overshot on price and complexity — and all of them demand your bank credentials. Many people just miss the glance: net worth and bills, at a look. This is that glance, rebuilt with privacy first.

  • Manual balances — cash, investments, and debts, entered by hand with full history charting. No bank login, ever.
  • Net-worth trend line — the direction and speed of your wealth, not budgeting depth you didn't ask for.
  • Bill list with due dates — what's owed and when, next to the numbers.
  • Home-screen widget — net worth and upcoming bills without opening the app.
  • Encrypted export/import — your data leaves the device only when you say so, and only encrypted.

Bill Reminder

In development

Nobody should pay a late fee for forgetting.

Android-first · One-time purchase

Thousands of people asked for a straightforward bill tracker for Android — and the alternatives either take a cut of your savings or want your banking passwords. This one just reminds you, on time, every time.

  • Manual entry — name, amount, renewal date, category. No account required, no bank linking.
  • Renewal calendar — every bill and subscription laid out where you can see them coming.
  • Smart reminders — nudges 3 days before and the day of, plus a morning-after note if something slipped.
  • Cost rollup — "you spend $X/month on subscriptions," computed on-device from your entries.
  • 100% on-device — your bills never leave your phone.

ADHD Focus Companion

In development

Help, never shame.

Android-first · One-time purchase

Standard productivity tools treat ADHD like laziness that needs more reminders. They fail on task paralysis, time blindness, and prioritization — the actual hard parts. This app acts as an external executive function: it decides what to do next, so you don't have to.

  • One task at a time — the app picks the single next thing. No overwhelming lists.
  • Real prioritization — due date, energy estimate, and your own ranked importance, combined with an explainable formula.
  • Adaptive nudges — reminders that adjust to your actual behavior. No shame spirals, no streaks, no red overdue badges.
  • Brain-dump inbox — capture everything fast, triage it later when you have the bandwidth.
  • 100% on-device — your tasks and patterns stay yours.

Home Maintenance

In development

Small tasks now beat big repairs later.

Android-first · One-time purchase

The incumbents are bloated subscriptions; the manual status quo is literally a Post-it note. Meanwhile the advice online is contradictory and nobody reminds you about the furnace filter. This is the simple checklist-and-log homeowners keep asking for.

  • Templated checklists — seasonal, monthly, and yearly tasks, templated by climate zone and home type. Editable, obviously.
  • Local reminders — "furnace filter," "gutter cleaning," "water heater flush," before they become repairs.
  • Completion log — every finished task logged with dates, exportable as CSV or JSON. Genuinely useful at resale or insurance-claim time.
  • Custom tasks & intervals — your house, your schedule.
  • 100% on-device — nothing uploaded, nothing tracked.
