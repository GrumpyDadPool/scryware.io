CashGlance

After Mint shut down, its successors overshot on price and complexity, and all of them demand your bank credentials. Many people just miss the glance: net worth and bills, at a look. CashGlance is that glance, rebuilt with privacy first. You enter the numbers yourself, and everything stays on your phone.

In closed testing on Google Play

Your money, on your terms.

Android · In closed testing on Google Play

Demo video coming soonA short walkthrough of the dashboard and bill entry will be embedded here.
  • Manual balances. Cash, investments, and debts, entered by hand with full history charting. No bank login, ever.
  • Net-worth trend line. The direction and speed of your money, not budgeting depth you did not ask for.
  • Bill list with due dates. What is owed and when, right next to the numbers.
  • Bill entry by description. Type a bill the way you would say it out loud, and Scryware Brain drafts the entry for you to check and save.
  • Home-screen widget. Net worth and upcoming bills without opening the app.
  • Encrypted export and import. Your data leaves the device only when you say so, and only encrypted.

Privacy

CashGlance collects nothing. There is no account, no bank login, no ads, and no analytics, and the balances and bills you type in never leave your phone. The full wording is in the privacy policy.
