HomeGlance

The incumbents are bloated subscriptions. The manual status quo is literally a Post-it note. Meanwhile the advice online is contradictory, and nobody reminds you about the furnace filter. HomeGlance is the simple checklist and log homeowners keep asking for, with reminders that land before small tasks turn into repairs.

In closed testing on Google Play

Small tasks now beat big repairs later.

Android · One-time $3.99 unlock. No subscription.

Demo video coming soonA short walkthrough of the checklist and the log will be embedded here.
  • Templated checklists. Seasonal, monthly, and yearly tasks, templated by climate zone and home type. Editable, obviously.
  • Plain-English task entry. Describe the job in your own words, and the app drafts the task for your checklist.
  • Describe a problem. Tell the app what looks wrong, and it suggests the checklist task that matches. Anything involving gas, wiring, structure, flooding, or mold gets pointed to a licensed professional instead of do-it-yourself steps.
  • Local reminders. Furnace filter, gutter cleaning, water heater flush, before they become repairs.
  • Completion log. Every finished task logged with dates, exportable as CSV or JSON. Genuinely useful at resale or insurance-claim time.
  • Custom tasks and intervals. Your house, your schedule.
  • 100% on-device. Nothing uploaded, nothing tracked.

Privacy

HomeGlance keeps the checklist and the completion log on your phone. There is no account, and the app does not send them to Scryware. The full wording is in the HomeGlance section of the privacy policy.
